=== English Detailed Description ===

Magic Tarot is a daily tarot divination extension with a dark, magical atmosphere. Draw a card each morning for guidance, or choose from more than 50 spreads when you have a real question about love, career, or a life decision.

Everything you need is in one place: eight beautifully designed decks, daily fortune readings, an AI deep reading mode powered by your own AI provider, a lucky number generator, and a decision helper for those moments when you simply cannot choose between two options. Every card includes detailed upright and reversed meanings, with simple, standard, and deep interpretation modes.

After a reading you can keep asking follow-up questions, and your reading history stays on your own device. The interface works in both English and Chinese, and the extension is completely free — every deck and every spread is unlocked from the start.

功能一应俱全：八套精心设计的牌组、每日运势、接入你自己 AI 服务商的深度解读、幸运数字生成器，以及在两个选项间纠结时帮你做决定的选择困难症助手。每张牌都附带详细的正位与逆位释义，并提供简洁、标准、深度三种解读模式。

解读完成后可以继续追问，占卜历史只保存在你自己的设备上。界面支持中英双语随时切换，完全免费，所有牌组与牌阵开箱即用，无任何限制。
