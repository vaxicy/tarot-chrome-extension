Privacy Policy

Overview

This Chrome Extension ("Magic Tarot - Daily Divination", "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our extension.

Information Collection

This Extension does not collect or store any personal information on external servers. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No Tracking: We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or any form of user behavior monitoring.
  • No Payments: The Extension and all its features are completely free. There are no in-app purchases, license codes, or payment flows.
  • Limited External Communication: The Extension operates primarily locally. External communication only occurs when you explicitly use the AI feature, and the request is sent directly to the AI service provider configured by you.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API and localStorage:

  • Reading History: Your tarot reading history is saved locally to allow you to review past readings.
  • Settings: Your preferences (selected deck, language, sound settings) and optional AI configuration are saved locally.

This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to the developer, and can be cleared by uninstalling the extension or using the "Clear History" function within the extension.

Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  • storage: Used exclusively to save your preferences and reading history locally on your device.
  • host_permissions: https://*/*: Required only when you enable and use the AI feature, so the Extension can connect to the AI service provider and endpoint you configure yourself.

Third-Party Services

This Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking platforms. If you choose to use the AI feature, the Extension communicates directly with the AI service provider and endpoint you configure (for example, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service). Your AI API key and requests are sent directly to that provider and are not routed through or stored on the developer's servers.

Children's Privacy

This Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the policy text and the "Last Updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/vaxicy/tarot-chrome-extension/issues

Compliance

This extension complies with: Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

隐私权政策

概述

本 Chrome 扩展程序（"Magic Tarot - 每日占卜"，简称"本扩展"）致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了我们在您使用扩展程序时如何处理信息。

信息收集

本扩展程序不收集或存储任何个人信息在外部服务器上。具体包括：

  • 无个人数据收集：我们不收集姓名、电子邮件地址或任何可识别个人身份的信息。
  • 无跟踪：我们不使用分析工具、跟踪 Cookie 或任何形式的用户行为监控。
  • 无付费：本扩展程序及其所有功能完全免费，不存在应用内购买、授权码验证或支付流程。
  • 有限的外部通信：扩展程序主要在本地运行。仅当您主动使用 AI 功能时，才会按您自己配置的 AI 服务提供商和端点发送请求。

数据存储

所有数据都本地存储在您的设备上，使用 Chrome 的 storage API 和 localStorage：

  • 占卜历史：您的塔罗占卜历史记录保存在本地，以便您回顾过去的占卜。
  • 设置：您的偏好设置（选择的牌组、语言、音效设置）以及可选的 AI 配置保存在本地。

这些数据永远不会离开您的设备，开发者无法访问，可以通过卸载扩展程序或在扩展程序内使用"清空记录"功能来清除。

权限说明

本扩展程序请求以下权限：

  • storage：仅用于将您的偏好设置和占卜历史保存在您的设备本地。
  • host_permissions: https://*/*：仅在您启用并使用 AI 功能时需要，以便扩展程序连接您自己配置的 AI 服务提供商和端点。

第三方服务

本扩展程序不集成任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪平台。如果您选择使用 AI 功能，扩展程序将直接与您配置的 AI 服务提供商和端点通信（例如 OpenAI、DeepSeek 或任何 OpenAI 兼容服务）。您的 API Key 和请求将直接发送给该服务商，不会经过开发者的服务器，也不会在开发者服务器上存储。

儿童隐私

本扩展程序不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何信息。

政策变更

如果我们对本隐私政策进行更改，我们将更新政策文本和"最后更新日期"。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请在我们的 GitHub 仓库上提出问题：https://github.com/vaxicy/tarot-chrome-extension/issues

合规性

本扩展符合：Chrome 网上应用店开发者计划政策、通用数据保护条例 (GDPR)、加州消费者隐私法案 (CCPA)。
